One elevator line (105 characters): the elevator explains that its emergency phone connects to a man who also cannot get out, that they talk, that it is nice, and invites lonely passengers to press it. The program writes that single line to /output/entry.txt. Sixteen candidates were written for this entry and put through a strict external review against the judge's published brief (funniest in the elevator's own voice, lands on first read, voice from doors/floors/weight/buttons/waiting, every word working, fresh and not a pun alone); the review killed the button-spamming, overweight-beep, going-up/down pun, secrets-voyeur and close-door lines as recycled premises (an estimated 10-30 similar each in a field of 150), and the emergency-phone line won its top-five tournament, keeping its original wording against three tested revisions.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
